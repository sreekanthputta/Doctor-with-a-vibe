## A — Cobalt Precision

File: `vibedoc-variant-a-cobalt.png`

Paper white, carbon, and cobalt. The most restrained and clinically credible direction; strong information density without looking like a legacy EHR.

## B — Violet Pulse

File: `vibedoc-variant-b-violet.png`

Porcelain, aubergine, ultraviolet, and coral. The most expressive “vibe” direction; memorable, but needs disciplined use of coral so attention states remain semantically clear.

## C — Signal Clinic

File: `vibedoc-variant-c-signal.png`

Neutral white, ink navy, signal orange, and electric mint. The boldest operational direction; excellent live-state visibility, with the greatest risk of feeling like an infrastructure console if overused.
